Seb Krier on AGI, Scaffolding, and Coasean Bargaining at Scale

In this episode of Justified Posteriors, we welcome Seb Krier — policy lead for AGI at Google DeepMind and excellent Twitter poster. Speaking in his personal capacity, Seb walks us through his understanding of AGI, why AI alignment has gone better than expected, the potential and limitations of a world where agents constantly barter on our behalf, and — of course — electronic music.

We also cover AI in London vs. New York, how Seb went from reading Marginal Revolution for 15 years to becoming a recurring character on it, and Seb’s side-splitting humor on mediocre AI conferences.
